# Fishing Points Growth Audit

**Live → https://eddieatadapty.github.io/fishing-points-flow-rebuild-v2/**

Five ideas to turn more Fishing Points users into subscribers, each built into a clickable version of Fishing Points's own onboarding. Prepared by Adapty.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Use the answers you already collect | +10–20% CR to purchase |
| 02 | Ask for the account after the purchase | +5–10% CR to purchase · ARPU up to +17% |
| 03 | Start with the free week switched on | +10–20% CR to trial or purchase |
| 04 | Show what Free leaves out, in your own forecast | +5–15% CR to purchase |
| 05 | Catch the people who tap Back | +5–10% CR from people who close the paywall |

Every price, rating and feature comes from Fishing Points's App Store listing, website or in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty’s expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across 30,000+ subscription apps, not measured lift for Fishing Points. They overlap, so they don’t add up.

Static single page, no build step: `python3 -m http.server`.
